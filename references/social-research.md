# Social discovery and research

Use this guide for Facebook, Instagram, Threads, public conversation discovery, and posts from people the signed-in user can legitimately view.

## First discover the available surface

Meta AI's ability to search social content varies by product surface, account, country, rollout, permissions, and the exact request. Inspect the signed-in Meta AI UI and available search tools before promising a search. Meta AI may be able to answer from connected social content in some contexts, but do not assume a universal index of Facebook, Instagram, or Threads.

When needed, use the relevant first-party app/site already available to the signed-in user. Do not silently switch accounts or use an unrelated browser profile.

## Search workflow

1. Clarify the target from the request: platform, topic/person, time window, geography, language, and whether the user means public posts or a friend's visible posts. Infer reasonable defaults where possible.
2. Search with focused natural-language queries. If results are broad, refine by exact phrases, topic synonyms, date, or platform.
3. Open candidate posts and verify that the post is actually accessible and relevant.
4. Capture only information needed for the task: platform, post URL where available, author/display name as shown, date/time if shown, key text, visible context, and visible engagement figures if relevant.
5. Compare multiple posts before calling something a trend. A single post is an example, not proof of a trend.
6. Summarize findings with links to source posts when available. Separate direct observations, Meta AI's summary, and your own interpretation.
7. If the user wants a plan or creative output, derive it from the verified findings and preserve links/attribution in the working notes.

## Friends' posts and privacy

- "My friend" means a person whose content is already visible to the signed-in account or surfaced by a supported Meta AI feature. If the account cannot view the post, stop and explain.
- Never bypass audience settings, private groups, blocked-account restrictions, login walls, or other access controls.
- Do not infer sensitive personal attributes, private relationships, location, health, or other intimate facts from a person's posts.
- Do not build dossiers or monitor a person's activity. Keep analysis scoped to the user's stated task and the minimum relevant posts.
- Do not copy private posts into public content or republish another person's image/video without considering permission and attribution.
- Do not like, react, comment, follow, DM, share, or otherwise interact with posts unless explicitly asked; confirm immediately before any consequential submission.

## Trend quality

A trend claim should be supported by multiple independent, recent examples or a clearly visible platform trend signal. Report the date window and the evidence available. Avoid equating likes with truth, quality, or broad popularity. Note when results may be personalized or incomplete.

## If search is unavailable

Do not fabricate results or claim Meta AI searched a platform when it did not. Explain which surface was unavailable and offer one practical alternative: search a first-party app the user can access, ask for a post link/screenshot, or prepare a research query for later.
